1 Welcome

This textbook accompanies PS50008A Research Methods & Experimental Design at Goldsmiths, University of London. It contains all lecture and lab readings organised by week, along with self-assessment quizzes to check your understanding.

2 How to Use This Textbook

Each week has two readings to complete:

  1. Lecture reading — Read before or after Wednesday’s lecture to reinforce the concepts
  2. Lab reading — Read before Thursday’s lab to prepare for practical exercises

After each week, test your understanding with the self-assessment quiz. These match the style of the MCQ exam.
