2 Conceptual Questions

2.1 Question 1: Why Research Methods?

Why is “common sense” unreliable for understanding human behaviour?

Common sense is unreliable because of:

  • Hindsight bias — events seem obvious after they occur
  • Confirmation bias — we notice supporting evidence and ignore contradicting evidence
  • Anecdotal evidence — individual cases can mislead
  • Common sense can justify contradictory conclusions (e.g., “opposites attract” vs “birds of a feather flock together”)

### Question 1: Why Research Methods?

Why is "common sense" unreliable for understanding human behaviour?

`r mcq(c("It's always wrong", answer = "It suffers from biases like hindsight and confirmation bias", "Scientists don't like common sense", "It only works for simple behaviours"))`

`r hide("Show explanation")`
Common sense is unreliable because of:

- **Hindsight bias** — events seem obvious after they occur
- **Confirmation bias** — we notice supporting evidence and ignore contradicting evidence
- **Anecdotal evidence** — individual cases can mislead
- Common sense can justify contradictory conclusions (e.g., "opposites attract" vs "birds of a feather flock together")

`r mcq(c("Saying 'not staring' when they were staring", answer = "Correctly detecting when someone IS staring", "Saying 'staring' when they were NOT staring", "Missing all trials"))`
