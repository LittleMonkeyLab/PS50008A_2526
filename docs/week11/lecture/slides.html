Welcome to PS50008A

Research Methods & Experimental Design

Term 2 | Spring 2026

What is This Module About?

You will learn to:

  • Design experiments that can answer your questions
  • Analyse data using R
  • Interpret results appropriately
  • Report findings in professional APA format

Why Research Methods?

Psychology is a science.

To make claims about human behaviour, we need:

  • Systematic observation
  • Controlled experiments
  • Statistical analysis
  • Peer review

This module teaches you the tools to do this.

What Is DataLab?

A series of 6 cognitive task stations where you:

  • Test reaction times
  • Complete memory tasks
  • Make estimations
  • Rate your confidence
  • Work in pairs
  • Record data in Qualtrics
  • Experience being a participant
  • Experience being a tester

Why DataLab?

The data you generate becomes our teaching dataset.

When we learn about:

Topic Your Data
Distributions Your reaction times
t-tests Room A vs Room B
Correlations Memory vs confidence

…we use your data, not abstract examples.

The 6 Stations

1. Simple Reaction Time — How fast can you respond to a stimulus?

2. Choice Reaction Time — How does decision-making affect speed?

3. Memory Task — How many items can you recall?

The 6 Stations (continued)

4. Estimation Task — How accurate are your judgments?

5. Group Estimation — Does group discussion improve accuracy?

6. Confidence Ratings — How well do you know what you know?
