Week 11: DataLab

PS50008A Research Methods & Experimental Design | 12-16 January 2026

Author

Gordon Wright

Published

12 January 2026

Overview

ImportantFirst Week of Term

This is your first session of PS50008A. There is no lecture this week—your first contact is the Thursday lab where you’ll participate in DataLab.

This week: Generate the dataset you’ll analyse throughout the term by participating in DataLab—a series of cognitive tasks and group activities.

Lab: Thursday 10:00-12:00 (two parallel sessions)

What is DataLab?

DataLab is a series of rotating stations where you’ll:

  • Complete cognitive tasks (reaction time, memory, estimation, and more)
  • Work in pairs
  • Generate real data about your own performance
  • Experience being both participant and tester

This data becomes the teaching dataset for the entire module. When we learn about distributions, t-tests, and correlations, we’ll use your data.

Before the Lab

NoteRequired Preparation
  1. Read the introductory lecture reading (10 minutes) — an introduction to the module
  2. Check your lab allocation (Lab 304A or Lab 304B)
  3. Arrive on time — we need to start promptly

- link to Lecture Reading

During the Lab

Part 1: Together as a Group

  • Complete demographics and initial survey
  • Enter the Mind Palace — a group-wide starter Activity!

Part 2: Rotate Through 5 Data Collection Activities (10 min each)

  1. Reflex — Data at the speed of mind!
  2. Time Warp — Doesn’t time fly, when you’re making data!
  3. Sixth Sense — you looking at me?
  4. Lady Luck — Who has the magic touch?
  5. Frontal Lob — Does anyone have a ‘hot hand’?

At each station, you’ll record your data in Qualtrics.

TipPre-Post Design

We’ll measure your mood before and after the stations to see if DataLab affects how you feel!

Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

  • Describe what variables are and how they can vary
  • Experience the difference between being a participant and a tester
  • Understand that the same measurement gives different results each time
  • Work systematically to record data

Materials

What’s Next?

  • Week 12: Your first lecture (2 hours). We’ll get out feet wet with the DataLab data together.

Key Concepts Introduced

  • Variables as things that vary
  • Within-subjects (pre-post) design
  • Sampling variability (two rooms, same protocol, different results?)
  • Systematic data recording
  • Metacognition and calibration
