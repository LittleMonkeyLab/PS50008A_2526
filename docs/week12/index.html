PS50008A Research Methods | 19-23 January 2026

Author

Gordon Wright

Published

19 January 2026

Overview

NoteFirst Lecture Week

This is your first full teaching week. Wednesday is a 2-hour lecture; Thursday is a 2-hour lab.

This week: Learn the complete research design framework — variables, designs, hypotheses, test selection, and descriptive statistics. All terminology you need for your Research Plan is introduced this week.

Lecture: Wednesday 10:00-12:00 RHBD 143 (2 hours) Lab: Thursday 10:00-12:00 WB304A and WB304B

Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

Variables

  • Define and identify independent variables (IV) and dependent variables (DV)
  • Distinguish continuous from categorical variables
  • Recognise when there is no IV (correlational design)

Design Types

  • Define between-subjects, within-subjects, matched pairs, and correlational designs
  • Identify the appropriate design for a research question
  • Match DataLab examples to each design type

Hypotheses

  • Write experimental and null hypotheses
  • Distinguish one-tailed from two-tailed hypotheses
  • Justify your choice of hypothesis direction

Test Selection

  • Know which statistical test matches each design type
  • Understand the difference between parametric and non-parametric tests

Descriptive Statistics

  • Calculate and interpret mean and standard deviation
  • Report descriptive statistics in APA format
  • Create summary data tables

ImportantRequired Preparation

This week answers the question: How do we design and describe research?

## Key Concepts

### Variables
- **Independent Variable (IV)**: What the researcher manipulates or selects
- **Dependent Variable (DV)**: What the researcher measures
- **No IV**: In correlational designs, we measure relationships without manipulation

### Design Types
- **Between-subjects**: Different participants in each condition (e.g., Room A vs Room B)
- **Within-subjects**: Same participants measured in all conditions (e.g., Pre vs Post mood)
- **Matched pairs**: Participants paired on key characteristics
- **Correlational**: Measuring relationships between variables (no manipulation)

### Hypotheses
- **Experimental hypothesis**: Predicts an effect or relationship will exist
- **Null hypothesis**: Predicts no effect or relationship
- **One-tailed**: Predicts the direction of the effect
- **Two-tailed**: Predicts a difference but not the direction

### Test Selection
| Design | Parametric Test | Non-parametric |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| Between-subjects | Independent t-test | Mann-Whitney U |
| Within-subjects | Paired t-test | Wilcoxon signed-rank |
| Correlational | Pearson's r | Spearman's rho |

### Descriptive Statistics
- **Mean**: The arithmetic average
- **Standard Deviation**: How spread out the data is
- **APA Format**: Report as M = x.xx, SD = x.xx
