Week 20: Wrapping Up

PS50008A Research Methods | 23–27 March 2026

Author

Gordon Wright

Published

23 March 2026

Overview

ImportantFinal week of scheduled teaching

This is the last lab week of PS50008A. Your Data Analysis I returns are on the site — use them to draft your Results section. Thank you for a brilliant year of curiosity, graft, and good humour.

WarningUpcoming assessment

Research Report — due mid–late April (exact date on Learn.gold). Start your write-up now if you haven’t already.

This week

  • Wednesday lecture — cancelled (no on-campus session). Instead, use Data Analysis I — Group Returns: preview images of the analysis on each group’s data.
  • Thursday lab (10:00–12:00, WB304A / WB304B): weaving analysis outputs (like the returned summaries / your own plots) into APA-style Results text, module evaluation reminder, and a proper send-off.

Learning objectives

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

  • Read your group’s returned analysis preview and extract statistics for reporting
  • Integrate numbers, tests, and figures into an APA-style Results section
  • Know where and how to complete the module evaluation
  • Plan a realistic timeline to finish the Research Report

Before Thursday lab

NotePreparation
  1. Open your group’s preview and note M, SD, N, test results, and what the plot shows.
  2. Skim the lab reading — Results reminders and report order.
  3. Bring questions about APA wording or your specific analysis.

The big picture

  • Returned summaries → Results section (APA prose + proper figures/tables)
  • Discussion still interprets meaning; Results only states what was found
  • Module evaluation — your feedback genuinely improves the module for next year

## Upcoming assessment
