Research Report

PS50008A Research Methods | Term 2 Spring 2026

Author

Gordon Wright

Published

12 January 2026

Key Information

Deadline Friday 1 May 2026, 12 noon
Submission Via Moodle submission link
Word count 2,500 words (±10%)
Format Individual lab report in APA format
Weight 40%

Task

Write an individual research report based on your group project data.

ImportantIndividual Responsibility

While data comes from your group project, the Research Report is an individual assessment. Each group member writes their own report independently.

Report Structure

Your report should follow standard APA format:

Section Content
Title Page Informative title, your name and student number
Abstract ~150 words summarising the study
Introduction Background, rationale, hypothesis
Method Design, Participants, Materials, Procedure
Results Descriptives, inferential tests, effect sizes
Discussion Interpretation, limitations, future directions
References APA format

Statistical Reporting

Report results in APA format:

  • t-test: t(df) = X.XX, p = .XXX, d = X.XX
  • Correlation: r(df) = .XX, p = .XXX
  • Include 95% confidence intervals

Report Writing Support

We build report-writing skills progressively throughout the term:

Weeks Focus
12-15 Results section (descriptives, CIs, test results)
16-17 Methods & Introduction
18-19 Introduction continued
20 Discussion section

Project Timeline

Week Milestone
11 Form groups (3-4 students)
12-15 Develop research question and design
— Reading Week — Work on Research Plan
16 Research Plan due; Begin data collection
17-18 Data collection and initial analysis
19-20 Complete analysis; Develop write-up (term ends Week 20)
Easter Individual report writing
Post-term Research Report due Friday 1 May
NotePost-Term Deadline

The Research Report deadline (1 May 2026) is after teaching ends. Use the Easter break to complete your write-up based on skills developed during term.

Assessment Criteria

Your report will be assessed on:

  • Clarity and structure of writing
  • Appropriate use of APA format
  • Quality of statistical reporting
  • Interpretation and discussion of findings
  • Critical evaluation and suggestions for future research
