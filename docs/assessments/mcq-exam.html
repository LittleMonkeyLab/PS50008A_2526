MCQ Exam

PS50008A Research Methods | Term 2 Spring 2026

Author

Gordon Wright

Published

11 January 2026

Key Information

Date Wednesday 11 March 2026
Time During your normal lecture slot
Duration 1 hour
Location Normal lecture venue
Format Paper-based, multiple choice
Weight 20%

Exam Structure

The paper contains multiple “Investigations” — each is a short scenario describing a research study. For each Investigation, you answer 6 questions:

  1. Design: Identify the design (between/within/matched/correlational)
  2. IV: Identify the independent variable (or recognise no IV)
  3. DV: Identify the dependent variable
  4. Hypothesis: Identify the appropriate hypothesis type
  5. Test: Select the appropriate statistical test
  6. Interpretation: Interpret the results

There will be a series of other MCQs testing comprehension of other aspects of the course to date.

What You Need to Know

Statistical Tests

You must be able to select the appropriate test:

Design Parametric Non-parametric
Between-participants Independent t-test Mann-Whitney U
Within-participants Paired t-test Wilcoxon signed-rank
Correlational Pearson’s r Spearman’s rho

The Decision Framework

Design → Variables → Hypothesis → Test → Interpret

How to Prepare

TipPractice Makes Perfect

Complete the weekly self-assessment activities - they are designed to be exactly like the exam questions.

  • Review the MCQ decision framework
  • Focus on the logic and the concepts being used, not memorisation
  • Practice identifying IVs and DVs from scenarios
  • Understand when to use each statistical test

Exam Conditions

  • Closed-book: No notes or materials allowed
  • No collaboration: Individual assessment
  • Paper-based: Bring a pen (not pencil)
  • No calculators: Not required for this exam
