## Outcomes
R5, R6, R7

## One-liner
Practice critical evaluation of published findings and methods critique for EDS Section B.

## Paragraph


## Activity structure
- 0-20 min: Critique exercise—spot the problems in example studies
- 20-40 min: Methods improvement exercise—"suggest two improvements"
- 40-50 min: Ecological validity exercise—redesign for real-world validity
- 50-60 min: Project analysis—groups begin/continue their analysis

## Skills practised
- R5: Critically evaluate published findings
- R6: Suggest methodological improvements (EDS Section B prep)
- R7: Discuss ecological validity (EDS Section B prep)

## Data used
Example studies provided (not Data Olympics)

## Software and tools
- Worksheet with example study descriptions
- Critique framework handout

## Instructions for students
Exercise 1: Read study summary, identify (a) what's good, (b) what's problematic, (c) what can't we conclude?

Exercise 2: Given study description, suggest two methodological improvements with justification. (Practice for EDS Q6)

Exercise 3: Propose more ecologically valid alternative design. (Practice for EDS Q8)

## Deliverable
Completed critique worksheet. At least two methodological improvements with justifications.

## Common problems
- Vague critiques ("sample size too small" without saying why it matters)
- Confusing ecological validity with external validity generally
- Improvements that don't actually address the study's weaknesses

## Demonstrator notes
This directly prepares for EDS Section B. Push for specificity—"what exactly would you change, and why would it help?" The EDS marking scheme rewards depth and justification.

## Project time
Groups should be actively analysing their data. Circulate and troubleshoot. This is the last lab before MCQ week.
