## Structure
- 0-10 min: MCQ format reminder—6 questions per investigation
- 10-25 min: The decision process: Design → IV → DV → Hypothesis → Test → Interpret
- 25-40 min: Walk through two practice investigations together
- 40-50 min: Common errors and misconceptions—"don't pick these"

## Key points for MCQ
Everything from weeks 12-17:
- Design identification (between/within/matched/correlational) [C1, C2, C3]
- IV and DV (or "no IV") [C5]
- Hypothesis forms (1-tailed/2-tailed) [C6, C7]
- Test selection [C14, C15, C16]
- p-value interpretation [C11, C12]
- Correlation ≠ causation [C13]
