## Outcomes
P1, P2, P3

## One-liner
Calculate descriptive statistics and produce visualisations using their Data Olympics data in R.

## Paragraph


## Activity structure
- 0-10 min: R basics reminder / WebR setup
- 10-30 min: Calculate mean and SD (by hand first, then R)
- 30-50 min: Produce histograms and boxplots
- 50-60 min: Project group time—finalise research question area

## Skills practised
- P1: Calculate mean and SD
- P2: Use R for descriptives (mean(), sd())
- P3: Histograms (hist()), boxplots (boxplot())

## Data used
Data Olympics dataset (their own data from week 11)

## Software and tools
- R/RStudio or WebR
- Functions: mean(), sd(), hist(), boxplot(), summary()

## Instructions for students
Worksheet with step-by-step R commands. Exercises: compute descriptives for RT, estimation accuracy, memory scores. Produce visualisations. Compare to lecture examples.

## Deliverable
Completed worksheet with R output. At least one histogram and one boxplot.

## Common problems
- Forgetting na.rm = TRUE for missing data
- Confusion about variable names in their dataset
- Boxplot interpretation (what are the whiskers?)

## Demonstrator notes
Walk through first R commands as a group. Let them struggle a bit before helping—productive struggle. Check everyone can load the data before moving on.

## Project time
Final 10 mins: Groups discuss what their DV will be and what level of measurement it is.
