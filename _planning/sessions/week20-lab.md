## Activity structure
- 0-20 min: By-hand calculation refresher—mean, SD, t-test or correlation
- 20-40 min: Section B practice—methods critique, ecological validity
- 40-60 min: Drop-in support for EDS and project write-up

## Skills practised
- P1: By-hand mean and SD (EDS Section A)
- P4/P5: By-hand t-test (EDS Section A)
- P6: By-hand correlation (EDS practice paper)
- R6: Methodological improvements (EDS Section B)
- R7: Ecological validity proposals (EDS Section B)

## Data used
Practice datasets (EDS-style, not Data Olympics)

## Software and tools
- Calculator
- Formula sheet
- Practice EDS questions

## Instructions for students
Part 1: By-hand calculation practice
- Work through provided dataset
- Calculate mean and SD for each condition
- Calculate t-test (or correlation)
- Show ALL steps (this is graded in EDS)

Part 2: Section B practice
- Write two methodological improvements with justification
- Write ecological validity proposal (aim for half a page)

Part 3: Drop-in
- EDS questions
- Project write-up questions

## Deliverable
Completed practice calculation. Section B draft responses.

## Common problems
- Arithmetic errors in by-hand calculation (check work!)
- Forgetting to show all steps (EDS requirement)
- Section B answers too brief or too vague
- Confusion about what "ecological validity" means

## Demonstrator notes
EDS due this week. Prioritize students struggling with by-hand calculation—this is worth the most marks in Section A. For Section B, push for specificity and depth.

## Project time
Those finished with EDS prep can work on Research Report. Circulate and support both.
