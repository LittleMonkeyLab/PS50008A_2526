# Learning Outcomes

Official module learning outcomes (from CCR):
1. Understand a range of key experimental design issues and statistical concepts
2. Choose the appropriate experimental design and method of analysis for a group research project
3. Analyse the experimental data using R
4. Write a formal scientific research report in APA format

---

## Conceptual (C) - primarily assessed via MCQ

### Design and variables
- C1: Distinguish experimental, correlational, and observational designs
- C2: Identify IV and DV (or recognise "no IV" in correlational designs)
- C3: Distinguish between-participants, within-participants, and matched-pairs designs
- C4: Identify levels of measurement (nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio)

### Hypotheses
- C5: Distinguish experimental vs correlational hypothesis forms
- C6: Distinguish 1-tailed (directional) vs 2-tailed (non-directional) hypotheses
- C7: Recognise operationalised hypotheses

### Inference and interpretation
- C8: Explain sampling variability
- C9: Interpret confidence intervals
- C10: Explain null hypothesis logic
- C11: Explain what a p-value does and does not mean
- C12: Interpret significance decisions (p < .05 logic)
- C13: Distinguish correlation from causation

### Statistical tests (conceptual selection)
- C14: Select appropriate test for design (t-test family, correlation family)
- C15: Distinguish parametric vs nonparametric alternatives
- C16: Match test to design: independent t ↔ Mann-Whitney, paired t ↔ Wilcoxon, Pearson ↔ Spearman

### Critical evaluation
- C17: Recognise p-hacking and questionable research practices
- C18: Interpret effect sizes
- C19: Understand relationship between sample size and precision

---

## Procedural (P) - primarily assessed via EDS

### Descriptive statistics
- P1: Calculate mean and standard deviation by hand
- P2: Calculate and interpret descriptive statistics using R
- P3: Produce and interpret data visualisations

### Inferential tests
- P4: Run and interpret a paired-samples t-test (or Wilcoxon)
- P5: Run and interpret an independent-samples t-test (or Mann-Whitney)
- P6: Run and interpret a correlation (Pearson or Spearman)
- P7: Run and interpret simple regression (extension)

### Software and reporting
- P8: Use R for data analysis
- P9: Check and report statistical assumptions
- P10: Report results in APA format

---

## Research Skills (R) - assessed via Group Project and Research Report

- R1: Formulate testable research questions
- R2: Operationalise variables (IV, DV, measurement)
- R3: Design appropriate methodology
- R4: Collect data systematically
- R5: Report results appropriately
- R6: Suggest methodological improvements
- R7: Discuss ecological validity and extensions
- R8: Work effectively in research teams
