## Constraints
- Week 11: No lecture (employability event) - video briefing instead
- Two parallel labs throughout (30 students each)
- Group project runs weeks 11-19, data collection ~weeks 14-16
- MCQ in week 18 means core concepts must land by week 17
- EDS requires by-hand calculation skills (mean, SD, t-test or Wilcoxon, correlation)
