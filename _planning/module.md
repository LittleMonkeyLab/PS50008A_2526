## Module Content (from CCR)
This module provides a basic introduction to how research is carried out in psychology in a step-by-step fashion. It provides an understanding of the thought processes and practical decisions that need to be made to carry out experimental research.

The module comprises:
- Lectures on different aspects of experimental design and statistical issues, and report writing
- Practical labs on research methodology, choosing and applying statistical tests, group work on designing experiments and collecting data

Students will learn how to choose the best design for a particular research question, and demonstrate their understanding of how statistical procedures can be employed to analyse data.

## Learning Outcomes (Official)
Students who successfully complete this module will be able to:
1. Understand a range of key experimental design issues and statistical concepts
2. Choose the appropriate experimental design and method of analysis for a group research project
3. Analyse the experimental data using R
4. Write a formal scientific research report in APA format
