## Week summary
From differences to relationships. Scatterplots first, always. Correlation as "do these vary together?" Pearson vs Spearman. The critical distinction: correlation is not causation.

## Key concepts introduced
- Correlation vs causation [C13]—this is the week to hammer it
- Scatterplots: direction, strength, form
- Pearson's r (parametric) vs Spearman's ρ (nonparametric) [C16]
- Interpreting correlation strength
- When there is "no IV" [C5]
- Restricted range, outliers, nonlinearity
