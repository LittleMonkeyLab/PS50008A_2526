## Week summary
Why does the same measurement give different answers? Introduction to distributions, central tendency, and spread using their own Data Olympics results. Shift from "what's the answer?" to "what's the range of plausible answers?"

## Key concepts introduced
- Distribution shape (normal, skewed)
- Mean, median, mode
- Standard deviation as spread
- Sampling variability: "if we ran it again..."
- Levels of measurement (nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio) [MCQ requirement: C4]
