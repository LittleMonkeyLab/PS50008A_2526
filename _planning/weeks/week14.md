## Week summary
The logic of inference: "could this difference be nothing?" Null hypothesis as thought experiment. The t-test as a formula shortcut for what resampling shows visually. Between vs within designs and their matched tests.

## Key concepts introduced
- Null hypothesis logic [C10]
- p-value: "how surprising if null true?" [C11]
- Significance decision at α = .05 [C12]
- Experimental vs correlational hypotheses [C5]
- 1-tailed vs 2-tailed hypotheses [C6, C7]
- Design types: between-participants, within-participants, matched pairs [C1, C2, C3]
- Test selection: independent t ↔ Mann-Whitney, paired t ↔ Wilcoxon [C14, C15, C16]
- Assumptions and when to use nonparametric [P9]

## Skills developed
