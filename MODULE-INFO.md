## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

1. Design and evaluate psychological experiments
2. Understand and apply descriptive and inferential statistics
3. Conduct correlational and experimental analyses
4. Report findings in APA format
5. Work collaboratively on research projects
